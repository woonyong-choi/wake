# Brand marks

`brand-glyphs.js` holds the official logos of the services Wake connects to, as each owner publishes them (colors, gradients
and proportions unchanged). They are used only to point at the service a connection, tool or provider belongs to. Wake is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of these companies or projects. Every name and logo is a trademark of its owner.

**These marks are not covered by Wake's Apache License 2.0.** The license grants no right to use them; each stays under its
owner's trademark and brand guidelines.

Only non-drawing parts were changed: ids get a prefix, class styles are written onto the elements, metadata is dropped, and the
root presentation attributes move onto a wrapping group. The Slack entry is the mark of the official logo file without the
wordmark; the GitLab entry views the press-kit icon without its empty margin. Black one-color marks (GitHub, OpenAI) take the
text color, black on light and white on dark, like the owners' one-color variants. Helm keeps both official versions (for light and for dark backgrounds) and the theme shows one. AWS uses the official
website header vector: a current-color wordmark and the original colored smile, with no background surface.

Marks with see-through inner shapes (the Prometheus flame, the GitHub Invertocat) keep them see-through, as the owners publish them: nothing is drawn under a mark.
